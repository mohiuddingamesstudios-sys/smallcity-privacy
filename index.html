Privacy Policy - Small City

Developer: Mohiuddin Games Studio. Contact: mohiuddingamesstudios@gmail.com

Small City does not require an account. Game progress such as money and car colors is stored only on your device. We do not collect your name, email or location.

You can reset your advertising ID in your Android settings.
